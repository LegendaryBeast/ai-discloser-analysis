# Style vs content classification rule (applied by one coder, 2026-10-27)

A candidate excess word is a **style** word if its meaning is generic: it could appear in an abstract from any field,
and it does not name a disease, organism, molecule, material, technique, measurement, statistical tool, place, topic
or AI technology. Verbs, adjectives, adverbs and connectives of evaluation, emphasis, transition and framing
(e.g. underscore, pivotal, notably, alongside), and abstract rhetorical nouns (insights, challenges, interplay,
advancements) are style words. Everything else is **content**. Borderline topical verbs used mainly with a clinical
object (treating, diagnosing, managing, restoring, suppressing) and AI-topic words (intelligence, generative,
writing) are content, to keep the index conservative. A second independent coder is planned for the full study.
